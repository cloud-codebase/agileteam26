🎮 Combat Controls

Two players duel locally on the same keyboard:

  • Player 1 (Left, Blue): A (Retreat), D (Advance), W (High Zasłona), S (Low Zasłona), E (Thrust)
  • Player 2 (Right, Red): ← (Advance), → (Retreat), ↑ (High Zasłona), ↓ (Low Zasłona), Space (Thrust)

Neutral stance holds the blade centered. Holding High or Low Zasłona raises/lowers the guard angle to parry incoming attacks!

⚔️ Fencing Rules & Zasłona Mechanics

Victory Condition: First fencer to reach 5 touchés wins the match, or whoever leads when the 3-minute bout timer expires.

Attack & Zasłona Defense:

  • Thrust (Lunge): Extends your blade toward the opponent's torso hitbox.
  • Zasłona (Parry): Deflecting the opponent's blade with your weapon prevents the touché and leaves the attacker vulnerable to a counter-riposte.
  • Strip Boundaries: Stepping beyond the warning lines onto the run-off limit warns or resets the fencer's on-guard distance.
